# Legal Timelines: Oregon (preservation and loan-book posture)

Statutory and regulatory clocks the agency-calendar and event-derivation rules encode, read from the seat of the housing finance agency, the city or county housing office, the PHA and the HOME / CDBG participating jurisdiction. **Verification status for this build: NOTHING in this file was read from an official source.** Every government host (oregonlegislature.gov, oregon.gov, hud.gov, ecfr.gov, law.cornell.edu, govinfo.gov, oregon.public.law) was egress-blocked; every row rests on search-snippet text of official documents and secondary summaries and carries `verified_live: false`. Nothing here is legal advice. The pack's `market-params.json` stores every day / month count used by `plb/agency_calendar.py`; the intervention catalog repeats the cite on every letter with "confirm current statutory text before sending". Buyer-side sections of the v2 file (ORS 86 / RCW 61.24 foreclosure, ORS 312 tax foreclosure, ORS 646 telephone solicitation, HB 4058 wholesaling, ORS 646A.702, rent-cap motivation) are dropped; a two-row book-risk stub remains.

## 11. Oregon Measure 50 (short)

Assessed Value is capped (MAV grows at most 3% a year from a 1995-96 base) and is commonly 50-55% of market in Multnomah; this pack carries no value on any lead, uses the roll only for exemption-code and delinquency events, and sets `av_is_market: false` so no downstream sibling scales AV.

## 12. Legislative history and what to read first
