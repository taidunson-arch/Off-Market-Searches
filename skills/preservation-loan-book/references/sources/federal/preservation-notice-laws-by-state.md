# Preservation-Notice and Purchase-Right Laws by State

State laws that require owners of publicly supported housing to give advance notice before restrictions end or a property leaves a program, and that give governments, tenants or qualified nonprofits a purchase right. In this pack the agency is the recipient of the notice and the holder of the purchase right: the table drives `PRESERVATION_NOTICE_WINDOW`, `PUSH_FIRST_NOTICE_DUE`, `PUSH_SECOND_NOTICE_DUE`, `TENANT_NOTICE_WINDOW`, `NOTICE_COMPLIANCE_BREACH`, `ROFR_RECORDED`, `THIRD_PARTY_OFFER_RECEIVED`, `ROFR_MATCH_DEADLINE`, the `designee_rofr` and `notice_compliance` routes and the `preservation_law` compliance gate. Oregon is the worked example; other rows are starting points assembled from prior knowledge and must be verified against current statute text before a pack relies on them (`verified: no` throughout; this build could not fetch any legislature site).

## How a pack uses this table

1. Copy the state's row into `legal-timelines.md` Section 1 with the exact statutory text once verified, and set `agency-profiles.yaml` `is_qualified_purchaser` / `is_designee` / `receives_push_notice` per profile from the definition of qualified purchaser and notice recipient.
2. Encode the owner notice windows in `dataset-schemas.yaml` `push.windows_months_before_anchor` (Oregon `[[36,30],[30,24]]`) and the due dates in `market-params.json` (`push_window_prep_months`, `push_first_notice_months`, `push_second_notice_months`); `plb/agency_calendar.py` derives `PRESERVATION_NOTICE_WINDOW`, `PUSH_WINDOW_PREP`, `PUSH_*_DUE` and `NOTICE_COMPLIANCE_BREACH` on `withdrawal_anchor_date` (restriction-type ends; annual HAP dates only with a non-renewal signal).
3. The purchase right is the agency's own right: encode the matching period as `rofr_match_days` (Oregon 30 days from the owner's certified mailing) and its duration as `rofr_duration_months_after_termination`; `ROFR_RECORDED` / `THIRD_PARTY_OFFER_RECEIVED` route `designee_rofr` with interventions `rofr_notice_recording`, `rofr_match_offer`, `rofr_assignment_memo`. Where the statute lets the agency appoint a designee on silence, `NOTICE_COMPLIANCE_BREACH` also routes `designee_rofr` for `is_qualified_purchaser` profiles.
4. Name the registry (or the agency's own log) in the pack `sources.md` and in `agency-profiles.yaml` `notice_log_source`; `PRESERVATION_NOTICE_RECEIVED` is RECORDED only when it comes from the agency log, a records request or a recorder instrument. Absence in a dataset is never owner silence: `notice_status` has three values (received, not_received_confirmed, unknown).
5. Tenant-notice periods become agency compliance checks (`TENANT_NOTICE_WINDOW`, `tenant_notice_check`, Req-IDs PUSH-03 / PUSH-04); where the statute's remedy for non-notice is an affordability extension (Oregon SB 973), the demand letter asserts the extension rather than any penalty.
6. Record the sanctions language exactly. If the statute bars sanctions on a withdrawing owner (Oregon ORS 456.265 title), no fines or penalties appear in any intervention.
