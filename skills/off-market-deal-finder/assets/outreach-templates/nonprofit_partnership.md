# Template: nonprofit_partnership

Archetypes: `lihtc_partnership_nonprofit_gp`, `nonprofit`. Decision maker: executive director, CFO or board chair (SOS officers; Form 990 Part VII). Route is `partnership_preservation`; this is a request for a meeting about capital and partnership, not an acquisition pitch.

Gates before use: `preservation_law`; `lihtc_tenant_protections`. No distress script, no reference to REAC scores, tax status or operating problems in writing, no acquisition offer unless invited.

## Meeting request (email or letter to the executive director)

```
Subject: Partnership on [property name] — recapitalization or preservation capital

[Name], Executive Director
[Nonprofit name]

[Company] invests alongside mission-driven owners in [state] to keep affordable housing affordable. We are reaching out because [property name] appears to be approaching [the end of its compliance period / its extended-use expiration / a Section 8 renewal decision] around [year], and owners at that point often need capital, a development partner, or a buyer who will carry the regulatory agreement forward.

We can act in several roles depending on what serves your mission: a recapitalization or resyndication partner, a source of preservation capital or bridge equity, a co-general partner on a rehab, or, where your board prefers to exit, a purchaser committed to a new long-term affordability covenant. If your organization holds a right of first refusal under the partnership agreement or IRC 42(i)(7), we are glad to support your exercise of it rather than compete with it.

Would a 30-minute conversation with you or your CFO be useful? We are happy to meet at your office.

[Name] | [Company] | [Business phone] | [Email]
```

## If `buyer_profile = nonprofit_preservation` or `qualified_purchaser`

Add: "Under Oregon's publicly supported housing preservation statute we [are / work with] a qualified purchaser; if a notice has been or will be filed on [property name], we can coordinate with OHCS and the city so that the preservation path stays open."

## Notes

- Nonprofit boards decide slowly; the cadence is meetings 4-6 weeks apart, not letters every 3 weeks.
- Bring something useful: a capital-needs framework, a resyndication sizing, a comparable preservation deal.
- Housing-authority-owned assets use `housing_authority_partnership.md` instead.

## Do not

- Pitch an acquisition price.
- Mention REAC/NSPIRE scores, stabilization awards, tax exemption status or delinquency.
- Approach staff below the ED/CFO or contact tenants or resident services.
