# Template: regional_operator

Archetype: `regional_operator` (sponsor family with several Portland-area entities sharing a principal office, registered agent or managers). Decision maker: principal or head of acquisitions/dispositions, from the SOS sibling cluster and the firm website.

Gates before use: `broker_exclusive` (never bypass a live exclusive on any sibling asset); `dnc_scrub` for business lines. Direct principal contact (email or call) is appropriate here; letters are optional.

## Email or call opener

```
Subject: [Property name] and your [metro] portfolio

[First name],

I run [Company], and we own [n] buildings in [metro]. I am reaching out directly because [property name] and [sibling property, if relevant] fit what we hold long term, and I would rather have a principal-to-principal conversation than wait for a marketing process.

Two things we can do that a listed process often cannot: close on a date you choose with no financing contingency, and structure around existing debt where the lender permits an assumption. If you are thinking about trimming the portfolio ahead of [year] maturities, or trading one asset to fund another, I would value fifteen minutes.

If nothing is in play, no follow-up from me.

[Name] | [Company] | [Business phone]
```

## Portfolio-liquidity variant (sponsor_loans_maturing_36mo >= 3)

Do not name the stressed asset. Open on the healthy asset you want; let them raise the portfolio. "If it would help to free up equity across the portfolio, we can look at more than one building."

## Sibling-sale variant (sibling_asset_sold_12mo)

"I saw [sibling property] traded earlier this year. If you are continuing to consolidate, [property name] is one we would want to talk about."

## Do not

- Name the sponsor's other distressed assets, lenders or court cases in writing.
- Contact during a live exclusive without the listing broker.
- Send retail-style letters to institutional sponsors (use `institutional.md`).
