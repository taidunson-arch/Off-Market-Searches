# Template: institutional

Archetype: `institutional` (REIT, fund, life company, pension or national operator holding fee or LP interests). Decision maker: asset manager or head of dispositions, from the fund website, SEC filings or CMBS Annex A.

Gates before use: `assumption_approval` when assumable debt is part of the proposal; `broker_exclusive`. Use their process (an IOI through the asset-management contact); no letters to a registered office, no price anchoring below their basis in writing.

## Indication of interest (email with one-page attachment)

```
Subject: Indication of interest — [Property name], [city]

[Name], [Title]

[Company] is a [description: private owner-operator / family office / fund] with [n] units in [metro]. We would like to submit an indication of interest for [property name] ([units] units, [year built]).

Basis for interest: long-term hold in a submarket we already operate in; ability to close without a financing contingency; willingness to assume the existing [lender type] loan subject to approval, or to retire it at closing.

Proposed process: a short period of confirmatory diligence on rent roll, trailing twelve months and the loan documents, followed by a PSA on your form. We are comfortable with a confidentiality agreement first.

If the asset is not being considered for sale, we would appreciate a one-line reply so we do not follow up.

[Name] | [Company] | [Business phone] | [Email]
```

## Notes

- Institutional sellers care about certainty, timing to fund closes, and clean reps. Lead with those.
- If the asset is in a securitized pool, the special servicer or master servicer may control consents; ask the asset manager who the loan contact is rather than contacting the servicer first.
- Price discovery happens after the NDA, not in the IOI.

## Do not

- Send a hand-addressed letter or call a switchboard asking "who owns this".
- Quote CMBS DSCR or occupancy from the tape back to them in writing.
- Approach LP investors in a fund directly.
