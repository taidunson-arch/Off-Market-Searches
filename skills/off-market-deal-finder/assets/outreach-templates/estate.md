# Template: estate

Archetype: `trust_estate` with PROBATE_FILED (personal representative or successor trustee after a death). All classes.

Gates before use: `probate_cooling` (no contact until letters testamentary / of administration have issued and at least 60 days have passed; 60-120 days is the window); `dnc_scrub`. Address the personal representative or the estate's attorney of record from the probate docket, never heirs directly when counsel is on the docket. Never mention the decedent's debts, the NOD, HECM, or taxes.

## Letter to the personal representative (or counsel)

```
[Date]

[Personal representative name], Personal Representative
Estate of [Decedent name], [County] Circuit Court Case No. [number]
c/o [Attorney name, firm] (when counsel is on the docket)
[Address]

Re: [Situs address]

Dear [Mr./Ms. Last name],

I am writing with respect for the work you are doing on behalf of the estate. I buy residential and small apartment properties in [county] and hold them, and I regularly work with personal representatives and trustees who need to convert a property to cash without repairs, showings or an extended marketing period.

If a sale of [situs address] is part of settling the estate, I can offer one buyer, one closing, and a date that fits the estate's schedule. I pay closing costs and purchase as-is, including contents if that is helpful.

If the estate has other plans, please disregard this letter; I will not follow up unless you ask me to.

Respectfully,
[Name]
[Company, if any]
[Business phone] | [Email]
```

## Follow-up (4-6 weeks later, to counsel's office by phone)

Confirm receipt; ask whether the estate has decided on disposition and whether a written outline (timeline, costs, as-is terms) would help the PR's decision; offer to coordinate with the estate's broker if one has been engaged (do not bypass a listing agreement).

## Notes for the analyst

- Stepped-up basis usually removes the recapture lock that kept the decedent from selling; the pitch is simplicity and certainty, not price.
- If a HECM is on record, the servicer's due-and-payable timeline (roughly 6-12 months with extensions) is the estate's real deadline; let counsel raise it.
- If tenants occupy the property, note that you buy with tenants in place and will honor existing leases and Oregon/Portland tenant rules.

## Do not

- Contact before letters issue or within 60 days of filing.
- Contact heirs when counsel is on the docket.
- Reference the decedent's debts, foreclosure status, HECM balance or tax delinquency.
- Use "we can stop the sale" or "avoid foreclosure" language.
