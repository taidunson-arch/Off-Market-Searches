# Preservation-Notice and Purchase-Right Laws by State

State laws that require owners of publicly supported housing to give advance notice before restrictions end or a property leaves a program, and that give governments, tenants or qualified nonprofits a purchase right. They drive `PRESERVATION_NOTICE_WINDOW`, `PRESERVATION_NOTICE_RECEIVED`, `ROFR_RECORDED` and the `preservation_law` compliance gate. Oregon is the worked example; other rows are starting points assembled from prior knowledge and must be verified against current statute text before a pack relies on them (`verified: no` throughout; this build could not fetch any legislature site).

## How a pack uses this table

1. Copy the state's row into `legal-timelines.md` with the exact statutory text once verified.
2. Encode `PRESERVATION_NOTICE_WINDOW` as `[expiration - first_notice_max, expiration - first_notice_min]` (Oregon: `[-36, -30]` months) and a second window where the statute has one.
3. Encode the purchase right as a routing rule: a recorded ROFR or an active offer period flags `rofr_encumbered` and routes to `partnership_preservation`; `buyer_profile = qualified_purchaser` or `nonprofit_preservation` turns it into an advantage.
4. Name the registry (or the records-request path) in the pack `sources.md`; `PRESERVATION_NOTICE_RECEIVED` is RECORDED only when it comes from the agency log, a records request or a recorder instrument.
5. Tenant-notice periods become underwriting constraints on conversion timing, never outreach talking points.

## Federal overlays that apply everywhere

| rule | effect |
|---|---|
| Section 8 opt-out: owner must give HUD and tenants not less than one year written notice (MAHRA / HUD Section 8 Renewal Policy Guide); contract administrator filing >= 120 days before expiration | `HAP_OPTOUT_NOTICE_DEADLINE = HAP_EXPIRATION - 12 mo`; enhanced vouchers for tenants |
| IRC 42(h)(6)(E)(ii) three-year decontrol after extended use ends | no good-cause-free eviction, no non-§42 rent increase for existing tenants |
| USDA 515 prepayment: RD approval, advance tenant notice (period per 7 CFR part 3560 subpart N; verify, usda.gov not fetched), restrictive-use provisions, tenant protections | `USDA_515_PREPAY_ELIGIBLE` is an opportunity for a preservation transfer, not a quick conversion |
| HUD Section 236 / 221(d)(3) prepayment and IRP decoupling through OAHP | recap path with use agreement |
